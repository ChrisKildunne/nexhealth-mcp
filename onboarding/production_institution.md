## Production Step 1 — Create a Production Institution

1. Log in to your NexHealth developer portal.

2. Switch to production mode by flipping the "Test mode" toggle at the top
   right of the page.
   NOTE: If you see a purple banner at the top of the page, you are still
   in test mode. Make sure it is gone before continuing.

3. Navigate to the Institutions page.

4. Click "Create Institution".

5. Fill in your institution information in the modal, then click
   "Save and continue".

Once your institution is created, continue to the production_datasource section
to connect Open Dental.
